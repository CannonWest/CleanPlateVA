Methodology & provenance

Score anatomy

Start at 100. Every violation subtracts.

The portal does not publish a usable numeric score. The raw 0–100 formula runs on every report; a letter grade is shown only when VDH published a broad checklist of at least 20 distinct applicable code items.

Computed here

Deduction per violation each block is one point off the score

Risk-factor violation Form items 1–29 — the practices most tied to foodborne illness: temperatures, cooking, hygiene, approved sources, contamination.
First time flagged −6
Detected repeat 6 × 1.5 −9
Good-retail-practice violation Form items 30+ — operational upkeep: cleaning, labeling, equipment, maintenance.
First time flagged −2
Detected repeat 2 × 1.5 −3

A risk-factor violation costs three times a good-retail-practice one; a detected repeat costs half again (hollow blocks). “Corrected on site” still deducts. Rounded and floored at 0.

Exact scoring edge cases

Repeat sourceA structured VDH Repeat flag or the word “repeat” in the observation makes that violation 1.5×.

Missing item numberDefaults to the lower good-retail-practice deduction of 2 points.

Corrected on siteStill deducts. The corrective action is shown separately; it does not erase the recorded violation.

Follow-up reportsUse the same raw formula, but checklist breadth—not the word “follow-up”—decides whether the result can become a facility grade. Their per-item IN/OUT verdicts also adjust the facility standing above, joined on the form item number.

Standing without follow-upsIdentical to the latest broad score. Items never re-checked keep their full deduction; undated or same-day reports never adjust.

04

Field-level boundary

What is official, and what is ours

Color is not the contract. Every group is labeled in words.

Official VDH record

Archived and reorganized

  • Facility name, address, permit type, and status
  • Permit and inspection GUIDs
  • Inspection date, type, and purpose
  • Violation item, citation, observation, and corrective action
  • Checklist IN/OUT/N/A/N/O, COS, and Repeat markings
  • Temperatures and inspector comments when present
CleanPlateVA-derived

Computed or enriched

  • Raw 0–100 formula on each report; A–F grade on broad assessments only
  • Facility standing: the latest broad grade adjusted by follow-up re-checks
  • Checklist compliance percentage
  • Broad-only score trend, red flags, and open-repeat count
  • Map coordinates and approximate-location flag
  • “Restaurants only” classification
  • Presentation merge of likely predecessor permits

Public finder

Identity, location, source handoff

Snapshot markers recorded as active at export time, names, addresses, geocoded coordinates, restaurant classification, approximation flag, and permit IDs. Status can age; presence is not proof a facility is currently open or permitted. No report dates, scores, grades, or inspection content.

Browser tries fullfalls back to finder

Authenticated archive

Reports plus derived signals

Whitelisted histories, citations, observations, checklists, temperatures, comments, status, lineage, and computed metrics from private R2 behind Cloudflare Access.

05

Limits & verification

What this view cannot promise

Transparency includes the edges, not just the happy path.

Selected coverage

Coverage is the ZIP count shown above, not every Virginia jurisdiction. Absence from this map does not mean absence from VDH; some places are outside the selected ZIPs or use other systems.

Snapshot, not live

Collection and publication can lag. “Newest report” is one maximum date, not proof that every covered area is equally current.

Permit status can age

An incremental scan can retain the last known “active” state after a facility stops receiving inspections. A full resweep reconciles closures; a marker is not operating-status proof.

Source retention

The portal typically exposes about two years of history. Missing report pages remain missing or unscored; this is an archive of what was collected, not a complete lifetime record.

Geocoded locations

Pins come from address lookups through Census, VGIN, OpenStreetMap/Nominatim, then ZIP-centroid fallback—not VDH coordinates. Some unbadged pins are street-level or interpolated, not rooftop; centroid fallbacks are labeled approximate.

Presentation heuristics

“Restaurants only” uses permit-type/name patterns. History merging uses shared location/address plus name similarity. Either can misclassify, combine, or miss a match; retained permit IDs are the audit trail.

Focused is not facility-wide

A focused follow-up stays visible as the latest event and adjusts the facility standing item by item, but it never replaces the broad assessment itself. Corrected-on-site violations still deduct from its raw formula.

VDH wins conflicts

Scores and summaries are comparison tools, not safety or illness predictions. If our presentation and the source disagree, the official VDH record is authoritative.

Verify at the source

Facility rows and report cards expose MyHealthDepartment links where available. Compare the observation, citation, corrective action, and checklist markings yourself.

Exact scoring edge cases

Repeat sourceA structured VDH Repeat flag or the word "repeat" in the observation makes that violation 1.5×.

Missing item numberDefaults to the lower good-retail-practice deduction of 2 points.

Corrected on siteDeducts 75% of its weight — the checklist's COS marking earns 25% back. The credit is provisional: a later re-check that finds the item OUT again revokes it. It never erases the recorded violation.

Follow-up reportsChecklist breadth — not the word "follow-up" — decides their role. Broad publishes a score; focused and scope-unknown publish null but can still adjust the facility grade item by item.

Grade without follow-upsIdentical to the latest broad score, banded. Items never re-checked keep their full deduction; undated or same-day reports never adjust.

Written-verdict follow-upsSome follow-ups publish no checklist — the verdict lives in the inspector's comments. Those are adjudicated into a machine verdict by a separate, audited step, then adjust the grade exactly like a re-check. A written verdict can restore or re-charge existing deductions, never add new ones; a structured re-check always outranks an earlier comment.

What is official, and what is ours

Color is not the contract. Every group is labeled in words.
Official VDH record

Archived and reorganized

  • Facility name, address, permit type, and status
  • Permit and inspection GUIDs
  • Inspection date, type, and purpose
  • Violation item, citation, observation, and corrective action
  • Checklist IN/OUT/N/A/N/O, COS, and Repeat markings
  • Temperatures and inspector comments when present
CleanPlateVA-derived

Computed or enriched

  • Inspection score: raw 0–100 formula on each report (no letter)
  • Facility grade: score + A–F letter, latest broad ± re-checks
  • Checklist compliance percentage
  • Broad-only score trend, red flags, and open-repeat count
  • Map coordinates and approximate-location flag
  • "Restaurants only" classification
  • Presentation merge of likely predecessor permits

05
Limits & verification

What this view cannot promise

Transparency includes the edges, not just the happy path.

Selected coverage

Coverage is the ZIP count shown above, not every Virginia jurisdiction. Absence from this map does not mean absence from VDH.

Snapshot, not live

Collection and publication can lag. "Newest report" is one maximum date, not proof every covered area is equally current.

Permit status can age

An incremental scan can retain the last known "active" state after inspections stop. A marker is not operating-status proof.

Source retention

The portal typically exposes about two years of history. Missing report pages remain missing or unscored — an archive of what was collected, not a lifetime record.

Geocoded locations

Pins come from address lookups (VGIN, Census, OSM/Nominatim, refined against Overture and Foursquare OS), with a ZIP-centroid fallback — not VDH coordinates. Venue-level and centroid pins are labeled approximate; some are manually placed after review.

Presentation heuristics

"Restaurants only" uses permit-type/name patterns. History merging uses location plus name similarity. Either can misclassify; retained permit IDs are the audit trail.

Focused is not facility-wide

A focused follow-up adjusts the grade item by item but never replaces the broad inspection that anchors it. Its own score is null; the OUT/addressed result describes that targeted visit.

VDH wins conflicts

Scores and summaries are comparison tools, not safety or illness predictions. If our presentation and the source disagree, the official VDH record is authoritative.

Verify at the source

Facility rows and report cards expose MyHealthDepartment links where available. Compare the observation, citation, corrective action, and checklist markings yourself.

Terms of Use and Data Acknowledgment

Inspection Records and Source Data

CleanPlateVA collects, organizes, and presents publicly available food establishment inspection records published by the Virginia Department of Health through MyHealthDepartment.

CleanPlateVA is an independent service and is not affiliated with, operated by, or endorsed by the Virginia Department of Health or MyHealthDepartment.

Inspection information displayed on this site is an archived snapshot and may not reflect the most recent information available from VDH. The original VDH inspection record remains the authoritative source. Users should consult the source record when current or official information is required.

CleanPlateVA Scores and Grades

Scores, letter grades, compliance percentages, trends, flags, classifications, and other summaries displayed by CleanPlateVA are calculated by CleanPlateVA from the underlying inspection records.

These calculations are not issued or approved by VDH and should not be interpreted as official restaurant grades, food safety certifications, health-risk assessments, or predictions of whether a person will become ill.

CleanPlateVA provides these calculations as a tool for comparing and reviewing inspection histories. They are provided as is, without warranty as to accuracy, completeness, or suitability for any particular purpose.
